# EchoMinutes — raw notes (illustrative / fictional)

Positioning: the "privacy-first, on-device" option. Big selling point is that transcription can
run **locally / in your own cloud tenant** — targets **regulated industries** (legal, health,
finance) and the privacy-conscious. "Your recordings never leave your boundary."

Pricing: "Personal" free for individuals (local-only, 1 seat). "Pro" $18/user/mo. "Self-hosted"
is a flat platform fee, quoted per deployment (one thread mentioned ~$2k/mo for an unlimited-seat
self-hosted instance). No usage-based minutes — it's seat- or deployment-based.

Features: on-device / VPC transcription, end-to-end encryption, redaction of PII in summaries,
BYO-model support (bring your own LLM endpoint). Weaker on integrations — Slack yes, but no CRM
sync, and the API is described as "early."

Chatter / sentiment: Privacy and security folks are **enthusiastic** ("finally, something legal
will approve"). The recurring complaint is **setup friction** for self-hosting and **fewer
integrations** than competitors. Summary quality is considered good but not best-in-class. Small
but loyal user base.

Funding: Leaner — a $4M seed round, ~20 employees. Bootstrapped-ish, grows by word of mouth in
privacy circles.

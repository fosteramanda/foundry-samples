# MeetScribe — raw notes (illustrative / fictional)

Tagline: "Enterprise-grade meeting intelligence." Clearly aimed at **mid-market and enterprise**
— lots of talk about compliance, retention policies, and admin dashboards. Sales-led motion,
"contact us" for anything above the team plan.

Pricing: no free tier. "Team" plan starts at $30/user/mo (min 5 seats). "Enterprise" is custom
priced — one procurement thread mentioned ~$45/user/mo at 200 seats with a volume discount.
Annual contracts only for Enterprise.

Features: everything NoteHive has plus — CRM sync (Salesforce/HubSpot), custom vocabulary,
role-based access control, data residency options (EU/US), and an analytics view ("talk-time,
sentiment trends across a team"). Has its own recording, not just meeting bots.

Chatter / sentiment: IT and security folks **love the compliance story** (SOC 2, data
residency, retention). The knock is that it's **expensive and heavy** — "overkill for a 10-person
startup," and onboarding "takes a quarter." Some reviewers say the summaries are more verbose /
less punchy than NoteHive's. Uptime is considered rock-solid.

Funding: Well funded — a $60M Series C two years ago; ~350 employees. Rumored to be nearing
profitability.

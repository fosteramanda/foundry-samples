# NoteHive — raw notes (illustrative / fictional)

Landing page blurb: "NoteHive records, transcribes and summarizes your meetings so you never
take notes again." Positions itself for **small teams and startups**. Big on Slack + Notion
integrations.

Pricing (from their pricing page, roughly): Free tier gives 300 transcription minutes/month.
"Pro" is $12 per user / month billed annually (or $15 monthly) and unlocks unlimited minutes +
AI summaries. "Business" is $25/user/mo and adds admin controls, SSO, and shared workspaces.

Features people mention: live transcription, speaker labels, auto summary with action items,
Slack digest after each call, search across all past meetings. No native video recording — it
rides on Zoom/Meet/Teams bots.

Chatter / sentiment: Reviews praise the **clean summaries** and the Slack digest ("the action
items alone are worth it"). Recurring complaints: **transcription accuracy drops on accents**
and the mobile app is "an afterthought." A few users grumble that unlimited minutes is gated
behind annual billing. Support response times get mixed reviews.

Funding: Raised a $9M Series A last year (per a TechCrunch-style writeup), led by a mid-tier
fund; ~40 employees.

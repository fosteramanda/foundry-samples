# RecapAI — raw notes (illustrative / fictional)

Positioning: the cheap, fast, "just works" consumer/prosumer pick. Freemium, product-led growth,
viral "share your recap" links. Targets **individuals, freelancers, and small teams** who want
zero setup.

Pricing: generous Free tier (600 minutes/mo, basic summaries). "Plus" is only $8/user/mo — the
**cheapest paid plan in the set** — unlimited minutes, better summaries, and export. No real
enterprise tier; "Teams" at $14/user/mo adds a shared folder and that's about it.

Features: one-click join, very fast summaries, shareable recap links, decent mobile app (the best
mobile experience of the bunch, reviewers say). Integrations are shallow — Google Calendar and
email, not much else. No admin/compliance controls to speak of.

Chatter / sentiment: Loved for **price and speed** and the **mobile app**. Complaints cluster on
**accuracy on technical jargon** and "summaries can be too short / miss nuance." Security-minded
buyers pass on it — "no SSO, no DPA, not enterprise-ready." Churn among power users who outgrow it.

Funding: Raised a $15M Series A recently on strong growth numbers; ~60 employees. Burn is high
(the free tier is expensive to run).

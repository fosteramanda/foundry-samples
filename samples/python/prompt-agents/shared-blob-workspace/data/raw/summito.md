# Summito — raw notes (illustrative / fictional)

Positioning: "meeting notes that plug into your workflow." The **integrations play** — lives
inside Notion, Linear, Jira, Asana, and pushes action items straight into your task tracker.
Targets **product and engineering teams** at scaleups.

Pricing: 14-day trial, no permanent free tier. "Standard" $16/user/mo, "Growth" $28/user/mo
(adds the deeper integrations + automations), enterprise on request. Middle-of-the-pack pricing.

Features: transcription + summaries are table stakes; the differentiator is **two-way sync** —
create a Jira ticket from an action item and it links back to the meeting. Automation rules
("if a decision is tagged, post to this Slack channel"). Analytics are basic.

Chatter / sentiment: PM/eng teams **love the task-tracker sync** ("action items actually get
done now"). Complaints: the **automations are fiddly to configure**, and the summary quality is
"fine, not amazing" — people use it *for the integrations*, not the notes. Occasional sync bugs
that duplicate tickets, which annoys people.

Funding: $25M Series B last year; ~120 employees. Growing fast in the product-team niche.
